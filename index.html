The question we are exploring

What still needs a human review?

A payment team needs to see which records belong together and which differences need follow-up. These are the questions guiding development.

  • 01

    Unmatched records

    Which payment, refund or settlement record has no supported link to the expected record?

  • 02

    Repeated records

    Is an entry repeated in an export, or is there evidence of a separate payment? Those are different questions.

  • 03

    Partial payments

    Which linked records account for only part of the expected amount, and what remains unresolved?

  • 04

    Refunds

    Can a refund be linked to its original payment without treating it as a new receipt?

  • 05

    Settlement differences

    Do fees, timing or missing records explain a difference between payment and settlement records?

Evidence with each review

Keep the record behind the difference.

A review needs the original identifiers, status, direction and source references. Matching amounts or dates alone cannot establish a link.

Relationships before guesses

The proposed rules use fixed identifiers and explicit links. They would not guess a match from approximate amounts or dates.

Unknown stays unresolved

Missing evidence would stay visible for review. A pending or failed payment would not be labelled settled, and a missing amount would not become zero.

A focused starting point

One workflow before broader coverage.

We plan to start with one provider, one chain, one stablecoin and one export format for a single payment team’s workflow. The provider’s export format and compatibility are unconfirmed.

  • We are checking the data contract and testing with synthetic records. We are not accepting customer data.
  • The proposed tool would review records. It would not move funds, sign transactions, issue refunds or automatically adjust your accounts.
  • This work does not establish accounting certification, regulatory compliance or any exemption from licensing requirements.
